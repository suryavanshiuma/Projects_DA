# SuperMart Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes **SuperMart sales data using Microsoft Excel** to understand overall business performance and identify key sales trends and contributors.

The raw dataset was already cleaned, so the project focused on **business analysis and reporting** using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and summary reports.

The analysis was structured around three major areas:

* Regional Sales Performance
* Finance & Revenue Analysis
* Sales Representative Performance

## Business Questions

The analysis was designed to answer questions such as:

* Which region generates the highest sales?
* Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
* How are sales trending month by month?
* Which cities generate the highest sales?
* Which products are performing the best?
* How much discount is being given, and where?
* Is discounting associated with higher sales?
* Which payment modes generate the most sales?
* How does sales performance differ across customer types?
* Who are the top-performing sales representatives?
* How does each sales representative perform based on sales and order volume?

## Analysis Performed

### 1. Regional Sales Performance

Analyzed **Net Sales by Region and Category** to identify the strongest geographical markets and understand category-wise contribution.

The analysis also included:

* Region-wise sales
* Category-wise sales
* City-wise sales
* City-wise discount amount
* Product performance
* Monthly sales trends

**Key observation:** The **West region** generated the highest net sales, followed by the South, East, and North regions.

### 2. Product Performance

Analyzed product-level sales to identify the products contributing most to overall revenue.

The top products identified in the analysis included:

* Iron Box
* Pressure Cooker
* Mixer Grinder
* Ceiling Fan
* LED TV 32in

### 3. Discount Analysis

Compared **Discount Amount with Net Sales** across cities and regions to understand where discounts were being offered and how sales performed in those markets.

The analysis helps evaluate whether higher discounting is associated with stronger sales and provides a basis for reviewing discount strategies.

### 4. Monthly Sales Trend

Created a month-wise sales analysis to identify changes in sales throughout the year.

The highest monthly net sales were recorded in **October**, while January had the lowest sales among the months analyzed.

### 5. Finance & Revenue Analysis

The finance report examined:

* Payment mode
* Number of orders
* Net sales
* Gross sales
* Discount amount
* Region-wise gross vs. net sales
* Customer type-wise sales

Payment modes analyzed included:

* Debit Card
* Net Banking
* UPI
* Cash
* Credit Card

The report also reconciles **Gross Sales, Discounts, and Net Sales** to understand the financial impact of discounting.

### 6. Sales Representative Performance

Analyzed sales representatives using:

* Total Net Sales
* Number of Orders
* Average Sales per Order
* Category-wise performance
* Order distribution

The analysis identified the strongest sales representatives and provided a comparison of individual performance.
